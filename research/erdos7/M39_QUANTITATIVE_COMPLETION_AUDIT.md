# Milestone 39 — audit of the quantitative Clique–Shearer lifting step

M35 and M37 use an ingredient that is stronger than the earlier qualitative
certificates: the summed full `rho` margin is treated as an actual lower bound
for uncovered mass.  This note isolates and audits that implication.

The conclusion is that the quantitative step is valid under the exact
completion hypotheses already checked by M16/M25/M37.

## 1. Set-up inside one staged fibre

Let `J` denote the four non-special prime coordinates and let `B` be the event
that none of the non-special support events occurs.  For every nonempty
`C subset J`, write `rho_C` for the Clique–Shearer coordinate polynomial of the
chosen upper-probability vector `q^0`.

Assume all `rho_C>0`.  Clique–Shearer with upper probability bounds gives

\[
\Pr(B)\ge \rho_J
\]

and, for `T subset J`, the relative bound

\[
\frac{\Pr(B)}{\Pr(B_{J\setminus T})}
\ge
\frac{\rho_J}{\rho_{J\setminus T}}.
\]

For the special support `{s} union T`, let `E_T` be its grouped event.  It is
independent of the sigma-algebra generated by non-special events supported
inside `J minus T`.  Since `Pr(E_T)<=q_{sT}`,

\[
\Pr(E_T\cap B)
\le
q_{sT}\Pr(B_{J\setminus T})
\le
q_{sT}\Pr(B)
\frac{\rho_{J\setminus T}}{\rho_J}.
\]

A union bound over all special supports therefore gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
\Pr(\text{avoid every event})
&\ge
\Pr(B)\left(
1-\sum_{T\subseteq J}
q_{sT}\frac{\rho_{J\setminus T}}{\rho_J}
\right)\\
&=
\Pr(B)\frac{\rho_{\rm full}}{\rho_J}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Whenever `rho_full>0`, `Pr(B)>=rho_J` implies the quantitative inequality

\[
\boxed{
\Pr(\text{uncovered in the fibre})
\ge \rho_{\rm full}(q).
}
\]

This is not merely a positivity implication.

## 2. What if `rho_full<=0`?

Then the uncovered probability is nonnegative, so trivially

\[
\Pr(\text{uncovered})\ge0\ge\rho_{\rm full}.
\]

Thus once we know that every fibre with `rho_full>0` lies in the non-special
Clique–Shearer region, the bound

\[
\boxed{
\Pr(\text{uncovered})\ge\rho_{\rm full}(q)
}
\]

holds in **every** selected fibre, including fibres with negative rho.

## 3. Why positive full rho forces the required region in M16/M25/M37

For the M35/M37 canonical branch the special singleton baseline is

\[
b_s=6/25,
\]

and the factor-5 box gives `q_s<=6/5`.  All proper non-special coordinate
polynomials are positive throughout the box; this is checked exactly by the
M16/M25 endpoint audit.

If `rho_J<=0`, then from the one-coordinate recurrence

\[
\rho_{\rm full}
=(1-q_s)\rho_J
-
\sum_{\varnothing\ne T\subseteq J}
q_{sT}\rho_{J\setminus T}.
\]

Using `q_s<=6/5`, `q_{sT}>=b_{sT}`, and positivity of every proper coordinate
polynomial,

\[
\rho_{\rm full}
\le
-\frac15\rho_J
-
\sum_{T\ne\varnothing}
 b_{sT}\rho_{J\setminus T}.
\]

The M16/M25 completion audit verifies that the right side is strictly negative
on the entire non-special box (its maximum occurs at an endpoint because it is
multi-affine).  Hence

\[
\rho_{\rm full}>0\Longrightarrow\rho_J>0.
\]

Together with the proper-coordinate audit this gives the full non-special
Clique–Shearer region required in Section 1.

M37 deliberately reuses `frontier_certificate(17)` for this audit rather than
silently assuming the implication.

## 4. From probability to deficiency

Each selected `3^4` fibre contains exactly `M` residues.  If a pointwise
certificate gives

\[
\sum_{r=1}^{41}\rho(q(r))\ge\eta,
\]

then the preceding sections imply

\[
\delta(3^4M)
\ge
M\sum_r\Pr(\text{uncovered}\mid r)
\ge
\boxed{M\eta}.
\]

The deficiency recurrence is exact:

\[
\delta(3^5M)\ge3\delta(3^4M)-\sigma(M).
\]

Therefore

\[
\delta(3^5M)
\ge
M\left(3\eta-\frac{\sigma(M)}M\right).
\]

This proves the M35/M37 lifting criterion with the correct sign and no density
1 substitution.

## 5. Audit scope

This note audits the mathematical bridge from the exact finite
Clique–Shearer/factorial certificates to the deficiency lower bounds used in
M35 and M37.  It does not claim that the full Erdős problem is solved, and it
does not replace execution of the exact certificate verifiers themselves.
